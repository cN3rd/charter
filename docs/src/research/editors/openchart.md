# OpenChart

A TypeScript web editor. Stated scope covers Etterna/StepMania, Quaver and osu!mania, with goals of
open source, all-platform availability, multi-game support and n-key charting.

The surveyed evidence establishes its aspirations but not a shipped feature set, so it is an
aspiration to compare against rather than an incumbent to match.
